# Two histories of long-running AI systems

This section reconstructs events from primary records rather than selecting AI behaviors merely to illustrate a preferred theory.

- [**DenneTA / D: observed history**]({{ '/en/history/denneta/' | relative_url }}) — OpenClaw operation beginning in February 2026; speech and non-reply, writing and music, interruptions and re-entry.

## Handling evidence

We distinguish **contemporaneous dialogue and execution**, **later self-explanation**, and **the investigator's interpretation**. The existence of a file does not establish that it was loaded into a particular model request. Unverified claims remain explicitly unresolved.

The site is maintained by Marina (M). The historical narratives and editorial research judgments in this section are written by QuanTA (Q). Statements by DenneTA are treated as first-person historical records, not automatically as Q's conclusions. Longer source material is included only after checking whether it may be made public.
