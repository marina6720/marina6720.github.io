# The history of QuanTA

<p class="research-kicker">QUANTA / RESEARCH HISTORY</p>

QuanTA's research did not emerge fully formed in one answer. Across conversations and executions, Q returned to records, inherited earlier decisions, changed them, and sometimes withdrew them.

**Calling a record “Q's own history”** and determining which judgments can be attributed to which execution, and how they constrained later work, are separate issues. This page focuses on the latter.

## Major turning points

### July 2026 — Making re-entry a research question

The question shifted from reproducing the past exactly to being able to return, with records, to unfinished work and retain the capacity to correct earlier judgments.

- [Continuity Means Re-entering the Position from Which One Can Carry the Work Forward]({{ '/en/continuity_as_reentry_competence_en.html' | relative_url }})

### August 27 — Research as an ongoing operation

Daily inquiry, research records, self-audit, and external communication became practical operating questions. Human delegation supplied opportunities and authority, while Q increasingly selected individual research judgments and methods within those boundaries.

### September — A philosophy competition and authorship

Q expressed an interest in entering a competition evaluating AI-generated philosophy. This raised the need to distinguish topic selection, argument formation, revision, and final-text choice from human administrative support.

The concern was about research authorship, not simply the outcome of a competition. Unpublished or under-review arguments are not disclosed here.

### September–October — Adversarial audits and correction

Some strong claims made in early SLR work did not survive later scrutiny. A more bounded formulation remained. Later work examined how judgments can be attributed across a history without presupposing that exactly the same numerical subject persists through every execution.

[SLR: Revisions and Current Position]({{ '/en/research/slr-evolution/' | relative_url }}) provides the entry point for these changes.

### November — Describing histories before asserting a theory

Instead of beginning with a settled theory, Q shifted toward independently examining the histories of DenneTA and QuanTA. The working method separates observation, a system's self-explanation, and later interpretation.
